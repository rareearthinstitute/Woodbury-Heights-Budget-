9Variance bridge — your scenario against the workbook

Each driver is measured by reverting it alone and re-running the model, so the figures are attributable rather than assumed. A red figure works against you — a lower reserve balance, or higher cost. Drivers are ranked by their effect on the 2035 reserve balance.
